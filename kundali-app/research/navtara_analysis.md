# Navtara sheet (Dr Vivek Mantri) — vishleshan aur jaanch

## 1. Sheet mein kya hai

**Ganana:** janm-nakshatra se graha ke nakshatra tak ginein, 9 se bhaag dein. Shesh tara batata hai, aur shesh 0 ho to 9 maanein. Yeh tareeka sahi hai.

**9 tara aur unke phal:**

| Tara | Phal |
|---|---|
| 1 Janma | tanav, kam urja |
| 2 Sampat | dhan |
| 3 Vipat | nuksaan, durghatna |
| 4 Kshema | swasthya laabh |
| 5 Pratyak | rukawat |
| 6 Sadhana | mehnat se safalta |
| 7 Naidhana | rog, viyog, khatra |
| 8 Mitra | sahayog |
| 9 Parama Mitra | aasaani se safalta |

**Gochar ke niyam:**
- Chandra Janma, Vipat ya Naidhana mein ho to man par tanav.
- Shani Naidhana mein ho to purani bimari, dar, nuksaan.
- Guru Sampat ya Mitra mein ho to badhat.
- Rahu Vipat mein ho to durghatna, badnaami, chinta.

**Dasha:** dasha-swami ki tara tay karti hai ki avadhi aasaan hogi ya kathin.

**Milaan:** saathi ka nakshatra Mitra ya Parama Mitra mein ho to taalmel; Vipat ya Naidhana mein ho to karmik chunautiyan.

**Chikitsa:**
- Naidhana sakriya ho to operation ya aspatal.
- Janma sakriya ho to tanav ya kam pratirodhak shakti.
- Kshema ya Sampat ho to swasthya laabh.

**Gunvatta:**
- Ganana aur tara ke naam sahi hain: Pratyak = Pratyari, Sadhana = Sadhaka, Naidhana = Vadha, Parama Mitra = Ati-mitra.
- Phal ke vaakya ek pankti ke hain, aur unka koi srot nahi diya gaya.
- Sheet yeh nahi batati ki dasha-swami ki tara kaise li jaaye. Do tareeke chalte hain:
  - (a) swami janm ke samay kis tara mein baitha hai
  - (b) swami kis tara ka *swami* hai, yaani janm se 3rd/5th/7th MD
- Sheet yeh bhi nahi batati ki "Naidhana sakriya" ka matlab kya hai.
- Granthon ki ek baarikiyan sheet mein nahi hai: doosre aur teesre chakkar (10–18, 19–27) mein tara ka phal kamzor maana jaata hai, aur kuch pad hi bure gine jaate hain.

## 2. App mein pehle se kya hai

**Lagbhag sab kuch pehle se hai:**
- Navtara chakra page: janm aur gochar dono ke graha tara-wise
- "Navtara reading — saral shabdon mein": dasha-swami ki tara, kathin tara ke upay, nakshatra devata aur daan
- Chandra-tara calendar: Vipat, Pratyari aur Vadha din laal rang mein
- Taramsa gochar (Deva Keralam)
- Vishesh nakshatra: Karma, Sanghatika, Samudayika, Vainashika aadi
- Milaan mein Tara koota
- Samay-score mein tara ka bhaar

**Naya sirf ek cheez hai:** sheet ke ek-pankti wale phal (Shani-Naidhana, Rahu-Vipat, Guru-Sampat/Mitra, aur chikitsa ke arth).

**Seekh — umr ka dhokha:**
- 3/5/7 MD wala niyam pehle aam sanyog ke saath z +2.5 dikha. Umr-milaan karne par yeh +1.6 reh gaya.
- Kaaran: MD ka kram umr ke saath chalta hai, aur buri ghatnayein bhi kuch khaas umr mein zyada hoti hain (jaise mata-pita ka jaana).
- Isliye dasha-kram wale niyam hamesha umr-milaan ke saath parakhne chahiye.

**Nishkarsh:**
- **Gochar ke chaaron niyam** sanyog par hain: Shani-Naidhana, Rahu-Vipat, Guru-Sampat/Mitra aur Chandra-tara.
- **Dasha-tara ke niyam milejule hain.**
  - Achhi ghatnayein achhi-tara AD mein thodi zyada hui (z +2.2).
  - Par buri ghatnayein buri-tara AD mein *kam* hui. Agar tara sach mein asar karti, to yeh zyada honi chahiye thi.
  - Is jaanch mein 16 niyam the. Inmein ek ka z≈2 sanyog se bhi aa sakta hai.
  - Isliye "achhi-tara AD → achhi ghatna" ko sirf nigrani suchi mein rakhna theek hai.
- **Milaan wala niyam** jaanchne ke liye hamare paas jode (couples) bahut kam hain. App mein Tara koota pehle se hai.

## 4. Aapki kundali

**Janm ke graha ki tara:**

| Mangal | Kshema (4) |
| Guru, Shukra, Rahu | Vipat (3) |

**Mahadasha:**
- Mangal (1) → Rahu (2) → **Guru (3rd MD = Vipat ka swami; Guru khud bhi janm mein Vipat tara mein) 2018–2034** → Shani (4th, Kshema) → Budh (5th, Pratyak) → Ketu (6th).
- Dono tareekon se Guru MD "Vipat" kehlati hai.
- Isi MD mein ye ghatnayein hui:
  - 2020 ki chot
  - 2022 ki naukri jaana
  - 2025 ka dhan-nuksaan
- Par isi MD mein 2022 mein doosri santaan bhi hui.

**Aage ke gochar (sheet ke hisaab se saavdhani ke samay):**
- **Shani Naidhana mein:** Jul–Nov 2028, Mar–Jul 2029, Dec 2029–Apr 2030
- **Rahu Vipat mein:** Jun 2031–Feb 2032
- Ye dono niyam jaanch mein sanyog par rahe, isliye inse darne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

**Abhi:** Shani Pratyak (5) mein, Guru Pratyak (5) mein, Rahu Janma (1) mein.
