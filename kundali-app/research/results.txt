Time-robust event-timing test (Oct 2026)
Data: 38 people, 335 dated events (11 exact birth time, 9 approximate, 18 date only).
Method: Moon-based + lagna-based dasha relevance (Vimshottari MD/AD/PD, Yogini MD/AD), AD-from-MD placement,
Jupiter/Saturn/double/node/Mars/Sun transits, sade sati, dasha x transit products; features averaged over the
birth-time uncertainty (exact: 1 sample, approx: +/-90 min in 7 samples, date only: 12 samples across the day).
Score = weighted sum, separate weights for positive and negative events; percentile of the event month among all
months of the person's life in that event type's age window (50 = chance, top-quarter = 25% by chance).
