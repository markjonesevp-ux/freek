### Nateeja
1. **Koi bhi niyam sanyog se saaf behtar nahi nikla.** Lagbhag 20 niyam parkhe gaye. Itne niyamon mein ek-do ka z = 2 ke aas-paas aa jaana sirf sanyog se bhi hota hai.
2. **KN Rao ka 5-mein-4 niyam pehli baar mein bahut achha dikha (7 baar laga, jabki sanyog se 3).** Lekin yeh jod maine pehle nateeje dekhne ke baad banaya tha, aur naye 38 vivahon par yeh 3 baar laga, jabki sanyog se 4.6 baar lagna chahiye tha. Sabak: koi bhi niyam naye data par dobara parkhe bina sach nahi maana ja sakta.
3. **Kitaabon ke kayi parameter lagbhag hamesha sach hote hain.**
   - KN Rao P1 zindagi ke 92–93% mahinon mein poora hota hai, aur double transit 71–79% mahinon mein.
   - Isliye "case study mein saare parameter mil gaye" kehna kuch saabit nahi karta.
   - Kitaabein yeh sanyog-dar kabhi nahi bataatin. Koi control group bhi nahi rakha gaya.
4. **Ek niyam par nazar rakhni chahiye: Guru ka gochar purush ke janm-Shukra / stri ke janm-Mangal par (BNN aur KN Rao P6).**
   - Yeh dono set mein sahi disha mein raha: kul 34/81, jabki sanyog se 26.9 (z ≈ +1.7).
   - Yeh abhi saboot nahi hai, lekin aage aur data par parakhne layak hai.

## 4. App ke liye sujhaav (aapki manzoori ke baad)

1. **Har samay-niyam ke saath sanyog-dar dikhayein.** Udaharan: "yeh sharth aapki zindagi ke 79% mahinon mein poori hoti hai." Isse upyogkarta khud samajh payega ki "niyam mil gaya" ka kitna matlab hai.
2. **Padhne ke liye naya content (sroton ke naam ke saath):**
   - Bhrigu Samhita lagna-anusaar 1296 phal (Hindi + English).
   - Jataka Parijata ke yog aur dasha-phal.
   - Essence of Nadi ke teen-grah yog.
   - Meena Nadi aur Nakshatra Chintamani ka nakshatra-swami phal.
3. **Prashna page:** Prasna Marga ka Ashtamangala prashna aur Daivajna Vallabha ke vishay-dar niyam.
4. **KP:** Krishnan Encyclopedia se naye vishay (bhaav-samooh).
5. **Research:** "Guru gochar Shukra/Mangal par" ko nigraani-suchi mein rakhein aur naye pakke logon par parakhte rahein.
6. **Na jodein:**
   - Brighu Prashna Nadi (107 − umar wala lookup).
   - Mundane jyotish.
   - Sansmaran.
   - Kharab OCR wali kitaabein.
