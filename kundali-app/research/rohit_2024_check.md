## 2024 ki baaki ghatnayein
Inke mahine pata nahi hain:
- kaam par adhikaar
- chhote bhai ki shaadi
- boss ka share mein nuksaan
- loan
- naye ghar ke baad parivaar mein jhagde

Varsh 30 (6th bhaav) ke engine ne inmein se kuch pakdi:
- vivaad/karz (no. 1) — loan aur jhagde
- Rahu–Guru yuti (satta) — share ka nuksaan
- bhai se matbhed (no. 5)

Yeh nahi pakda:
- kaam par adhikaar — engine ne career ko "savdhani" bataya
- ghar/car ki kharid

## Ek note
KP engine pehle Rohit ki kundali ki in ghatnaon par tune hua tha: durghatna, padhai, vivah, career, santaan, naukri, dhan-haani. Ghar aur car us list mein nahi the. Isliye ghar par KP ke 88% aur 75% us tuning se nahi aaye.

Par yeh sirf 3 ghatnayein hain, aur KP 40 logon par sanyog jitna hi aaya tha. Inhe `dobset.json` mein Rohit ki ghatnaon mein joD diya gaya hai.
